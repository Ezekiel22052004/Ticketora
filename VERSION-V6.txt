Ticketora V6 — Payment + Customer Fees Fix

- Tchin fees are charged to the customer (fees_on_customer=true).
- Ticket price remains the organizer's ticket amount.
- Customer payment is ticket amount + 5% fee.
- Payment return keeps the Ticketora order reference even if TCHIN_RETURN_URL is configured.
- Payment confirmation accepts provider amount as base or gross amount (base + 5% customer fee).
- Existing V5 payment recovery by reference is preserved.
- Ticket creation and organizer/admin revenue split remain based on the ticket amount, not Tchin fees.
- Cagnotte payments also use customer-paid Tchin fees.
- Fixed ensureEventImageColumn syntax from the previous package.
